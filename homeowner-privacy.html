Homeowner Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This policy applies when you ask for a quote or information through an ad, form, website, map listing or phone number run by Perry Holding Group, LLC d/b/a Everpointe Media ("we," "us"). That includes our home-services brands AirSeal Insulation Co., Ironclad Floor Coatings, Bedrock Concrete Co., Boundary Fence Co., Evergreen Turf Co. and Glowline Lighting Co., and the local service websites we operate.

The short version: we connect homeowners with one trusted, independent local contractor for the service they asked about. When you request a quote, we pass your request to that one contractor so they can contact you. We don't sell your information to data brokers or send it to multiple contractors.

1. Information we collect

  • What you give us: your name and phone number, your email if you provide it, your answers to project questions (for example the type of project and timeline), and your city or ZIP code.
  • Calls and texts: when you call or text one of our numbers, we receive your phone number, the time and length of the call, and the message content. Calls may be recorded for quality and to verify that a request was genuine.
  • From ad platforms: if you submit a Facebook or Instagram lead form, Meta sends us the information you entered.
  • Automatically: our websites use cookies and similar tools (such as the Meta Pixel and Google tags) that collect device, browser and page-visit information.

2. How we use it

  • To pass your request to a local contractor who serves your area and offers the service you asked about.
  • To contact you about your request by phone, text or email, including a confirmation text right after you submit.
  • To confirm requests are genuine and keep spam out.
  • To measure and improve our advertising. For example, we may tell ad platforms (Meta, Google) that a request was submitted or that an estimate was booked, so their ads reach people more likely to need the service.

3. Who we share it with

  • One local partner contractor: the independent business that serves your area for the service you requested. That contractor uses your information to contact you, give you a quote and do the work if you hire them. Their own privacy practices apply to what they do with it.
  • Service providers that help us run our business, such as our CRM and phone/text provider (HighLevel and its carriers), website hosting, and ad platforms. They may use the information only to provide services to us.
  • Legal reasons: when required by law, or to protect rights, safety or property.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share mobile phone numbers or text-messaging opt-in data with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing.

4. Calls and text messages

When you submit a request, you agree that we and the partner contractor serving your area may contact you at the number you gave about your request, including by calls and text messages that may be sent with automated technology. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. Message frequency varies with your request. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop texts, or HELP for help. You can also email us to opt out.

5. Cookies and ad tracking

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. You can control ad personalization in your Facebook ad preferences and Google ad settings.

6. How long we keep it

We keep request records for as long as we need them to deliver your request, handle billing and disputes with our partner contractors, and meet legal obligations, generally up to 3 years. After that we delete them or make them anonymous.

7. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to tell you what information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can opt out of further contact at any time. Email michael@everpointemedia.com with the phone number you used. Depending on your state, you may have additional rights, and we'll honor them.

8. Children

Our services are for adult homeowners. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

9. Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, but no system is perfectly secure.

10. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date at the top when we do.
