Questions

Straight Answers

Is there a contract or retainer?

No. There is no retainer and no long-term contract. You pay for qualified leads and can raise, lower or pause volume at any time.

Are the leads exclusive?

Yes. Each service area is limited to one company, and a lead is never resold to another contractor.

What if the leads don't show up?

Your first 10 leads are guaranteed within 6 weeks of payment. If we fall short, we refund every lead we didn't deliver.

How do I know the leads are real?

Every lead is logged with its source, time and contact details, and tracked calls record their length. You see the same data we do. Anything invalid is refunded.

Who handles the ads and the website?

We do. We build the landing pages, run the Google and Meta campaigns, and own the tracking. You focus on quoting and closing.

How fast can we start?

Most service areas go live within 48 hours of signup, and most first leads arrive within one to three weeks.
