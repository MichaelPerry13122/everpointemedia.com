About

Built Around One Idea: Get Paid Only When You Do.

Contractors have been burned by agencies that charge a retainer, send a report, and leave the phone silent. We built Everpointe Media to flip that: we take the risk, and you pay only for qualified leads we deliver.

We run the ads, build the funnels, track every call and form, and send exclusive homeowner leads to one contractor per service area. You quote and close. If a lead is bad, you don't pay for it.

Why pay-per-lead works

  • Aligned incentives. We only earn when qualified leads reach you, so we work to make them good.
  • No retainer or contract. Raise, lower or pause volume any time.
  • Exclusive. One company per service area, never resold.
  • Fully transparent. Every lead is logged and itemized, so you can verify everything.

Who we work with

High-ticket home-service contractors, with deep experience in concrete, epoxy and spray foam. Single-owner shops and multi-location companies alike.

The founder

Michael Perry — Founder and operator of Everpointe Media. Background in door-to-door sales, applied math and local lead generation. He built Everpointe to make paid lead generation low-risk for the contractors who do the work.
