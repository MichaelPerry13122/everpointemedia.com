How It Works

We Do The Marketing. You Do The Jobs.

We don't sell hours, posts or impressions. We deliver qualified homeowner leads in your exclusive service area, and you only pay for the ones we send.

⌖

Exclusive Territory

Each service area is limited to one company. Your leads are never resold or shared with a competitor.

▤

Landing Pages & Forms

High-converting pages and short lead forms built for each trade, with the homeowner's request shared with your company by name.

◎

Google & Meta Ads

We build, fund and manage the campaigns, and filter out DIY, job-seeker and out-of-area traffic so you get project buyers.

☎

Call & Form Tracking

Dedicated tracking numbers and form routing log every call and request with its source, time and length.

✉

Instant Lead Delivery

Push alerts to your phone, a fast auto-text to the homeowner in your company's name, and a booking link, so you can reach them within minutes.

≡

CRM & Reporting

Every lead lands in your CRM pipeline. Weekly itemized billing shows exactly what you were charged for.

The Fine Print, Made Simple

Qualified Leads & Refunds

A qualified lead is

  • A real name and a working phone number
  • Inside your service area
  • Asking about a service you offer

We refund

  • Disconnected or wrong numbers, spam and duplicates
  • Out-of-area or wrong-service requests
  • Half the price for leads that never answer after your logged follow-up

Full terms are set out in the written Lead Partner Agreement. Pricing varies by trade and service area and is confirmed before you start.
