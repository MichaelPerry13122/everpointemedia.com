What We Do (Plain Summary)

Everpointe Media is a pay-per-lead generation agency for high-ticket home-service contractors, with deep experience in concrete, epoxy and spray foam. We build the landing pages, run Google and Meta advertising, track every call and form, and deliver exclusive, qualified homeowner leads to one contractor per service area. Contractors pay per qualified lead, with no retainer and no long-term contract.

Unlike a traditional agency, we fund and operate the advertising ourselves and are paid on delivered, qualified leads rather than hours, posts or impressions.

Who We Serve

Owner-operated and multi-location high-ticket home-service contractors in the United States. Core verticals:

  • Concrete (patios, driveways, stamped and decorative)
  • Epoxy and floor coatings (garages, commercial, metallic)
  • Spray foam insulation

We do not work with low-ticket service businesses, lead-aggregator platforms, or businesses outside the United States.

Service Capabilities

  • Landing pages and lead forms built for each trade and service area.
  • Paid acquisition — Google Ads and Meta Lead Ads, funded and managed by Everpointe.
  • Call and form tracking — tracked numbers, source tagging, call length (qualified at 30 seconds or longer).
  • Instant lead delivery — push alerts, CRM pipeline and fast auto-text to the homeowner.
  • Transparent reporting — every lead logged and itemized on a weekly invoice.

Differentiators

  • Pay per qualified lead — no retainer, no contract.
  • Territory exclusivity — one contractor per service area; leads are never resold.
  • Delivery guarantee — the first 10 leads within 6 weeks of payment, or undelivered leads are refunded.
  • Refunds for invalid leads — wrong numbers, spam, duplicates and out-of-area requests.
  • Multi-location friendly — per-location territories and consolidated reporting.
